## Hi, I'm Bastian — online, Fokuspunk.

I'm **Bastian Machek**, a photographer and free-software developer in the **Landshut area of southern Germany**. You may have found me as **Fokuspunk** through photography, as **bmachek** on GitHub, or through one of my Lightroom plugins. Those are all me.

“Doing stuff with cameras and computers” is the short version. The longer version is a collection of projects that connect the things I enjoy: taking photographs, building tools, and experimenting with what software can do.

## Photography meets software

I photograph with a **Canon EOS R5 Mark II** and a collection of wide-angle, standard, macro and telephoto lenses. My [gear page]({{ '/gear/' | relative_url }}) lists the equipment and software I use; my photographs are at [fokuspunk.de](https://www.fokuspunk.de) and on [Instagram as @fokuspunk](https://www.instagram.com/fokuspunk/).

The software side reaches into that same workflow. Lightroom Classic is where I organise and process photographs. My projects extend what happens around the catalog: describing pictures, finding them again, keeping a self-hosted photo library in sync, and delivering galleries.

## Projects and my role in them

I'm the **creator and lead developer of [LrGeniusAI]({{ '/LrGeniusAI/' | relative_url }})**, developed under the [LrGenius organisation](https://github.com/LrGenius). It brings AI tagging, image descriptions and natural-language search to Lightroom Classic, alongside face recognition and on-device species identification. It supports built-in local AI as well as cloud models. The plugin uses Lua, with a local Rust backend.

The **[Lightroom Immich Plugin]({{ '/lrc-immich-plugin/' | relative_url }})** connects Lightroom Classic to Immich for export, publishing and import workflows. I also wrote the **initial implementation of the [Lightroom PicPeak Plugin]({{ '/lrc-picpeak/' | relative_url }})**, now hosted under the PicPeak organisation. Its current workflow includes bringing client selections back to matching RAW files.

**[social-mcp]({{ '/social-mcp/' | relative_url }})** connects a self-hosted publishing workflow to an AI assistant, with tools for Instagram and Facebook Pages, scheduling and analytics.

My newer project, **[MoodSwings — Die Stadt der Flummis]({{ '/moodswings/' | relative_url }})**, heads in a different direction: an open-world comedy game built in Rust and Bevy. Its residents bounce, react to provocation, and catch each other's moods. The Landshüpf variant uses real map data from Landshut.

## A personal reason to build CIDP Buddy

I'm married and a father. I also live with **CIDP — Chronic Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy**, a rare autoimmune nerve disease.

That experience is behind **[CIDP Buddy]({{ '/cidp-buddy/' | relative_url }})**. I built the app to help organise infusions, medication, supplies and schedules, with a diary for vitals and symptoms. It is a practical project rooted in my own day-to-day life.
